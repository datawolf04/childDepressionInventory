# CDI Scoring Guide: Children's Depression Inventory

**Source Document:** Kovacs, M., & MHS Staff. (2019). *Children's Depression Inventory 2nd Edition (CDI 2)* [Product overview]. American Psychological Association. https://www.apa.org/obesity-guideline/depression-inventory.pdf

This guide covers scoring for both the original CDI (27-item) and the CDI 2 (28-item). The CDI is a screening tool only; it does not yield a clinical diagnosis of depression. Positive screens must be followed by a structured diagnostic interview (Fristad et al., 1997).

---

## 1. Forms Overview

| Form | Rater | Items | Age Range | Administration Time |
|------|-------|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| CDI:SR (original) | Self-report | 27 | 7–17 | 15 min |
| CDI 2:SR (full-length) | Self-report | 28 | 7–17 | 10–15 min |
| CDI 2:SR[S] (short) | Self-report | 12 | 7–17 | 5–10 min |
| CDI 2:P | Parent/caregiver | 17 | 7–17 | 5–10 min |
| CDI 2:T | Teacher | 12 | 7–17 | 5–10 min |

(Kovacs, 1992; Kovacs & MHS Staff, 2011)

---

## 2. Item Scoring

### Self-Report Forms (original CDI and CDI 2:SR)
Each item presents three statements reflecting increasing symptom severity:

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 0 | Absence of symptom |
| 1 | Mild or probable symptom |
| 2 | Definite symptom |

The child selects the statement that best describes feelings or behaviors over the **past two weeks**.

### Parent / Teacher Forms (CDI 2:P and CDI 2:T)
Items use a 4-point scale:

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 0 | Not at all |
| 1 | Some of the time |
| 2 | Often |
| 3 | Most of the time |

(Kovacs & MHS Staff, 2011)

---

## 3. Computing Scores

### 3A. Original CDI (27-item, Kovacs, 1992)

**Total Score:** Sum of all 27 items. Range: 0–54.

**Five Subscales** (per Kovacs's original factor structure):

| Subscale | Items | Score Range | Construct |
|----------|-------|-------------|-----------|
| Negative Mood | 6 | 0–12 | Sadness, crying, self-blame, worrying, loneliness, feeling unloved |
| Interpersonal Problems | 4 | 0–8 | Misbehavior, disobedience, fighting |
| Ineffectiveness | 4 | 0–8 | Self-criticism, indecisiveness, schoolwork difficulty, poor school performance |
| Anhedonia | 8 | 0–16 | Loss of pleasure, sleep disturbance, fatigue, appetite loss, social withdrawal, school dislike |
| Negative Self-Esteem | 5 | 0–10 | Pessimism, suicidal ideation, negative body image, self-depreciation |

**Note:** The exact item-to-subscale assignment is proprietary to the CDI manual (Multi-Health Systems). The subscale item counts above reflect Kovacs's original five-factor model (Kovacs, 1992; Saylor et al., 1984; Jelínek et al., 2021).

Subscale scores are computed by summing the items assigned to each subscale.

### 3B. CDI 2 Self-Report — Full-Length (28-item, Kovacs & MHS Staff, 2011)

**Total Score:** Sum of all 28 items. Range: 0–56.

**Two Scale Scores:**

| Scale | Items | Score Range | Construct |
|-------|-------|-------------|-----------|
| Emotional Problems | 15 | 0–30 | Internalizing affective symptoms |
| Functional Problems | 13 | 0–26 | Functional impairment in social and academic domains |

**Four Subscale Scores** (nested under scales):

| Subscale | Scale | Items | Score Range |
|----------|-------|-------|-------------|
| Negative Mood / Physical Symptoms | Emotional Problems | 9 | 0–18 |
| Negative Self-Esteem | Emotional Problems | 6 | 0–12 |
| Interpersonal Problems | Functional Problems | 5 | 0–10 |
| Ineffectiveness | Functional Problems | 8 | 0–16 |

Scale and subscale scores are computed by summing the relevant item scores as specified in the CDI 2 manual (Kovacs & MHS Staff, 2011).

### 3C. CDI 2 Self-Report — Short Form (12-item)

**Total Score only:** Sum of 12 items. Range: 0–24. No subscale scores are computed for the short form. The total score is designed to be comparable to the full-length version (Kovacs & MHS Staff, 2011).

### 3D. CDI 2 Parent and Teacher Forms

| Form | Score Type | Items | Range |
|------|-----------|-------|-------|
| CDI 2:P (Parent) | Total Score | 17 | 0–51 |
| CDI 2:P (Parent) | Emotional Problems scale | varies | — |
| CDI 2:P (Parent) | Functional Problems scale | varies | — |
| CDI 2:T (Teacher) | Total Score | 12 | 0–36 |
| CDI 2:T (Teacher) | Emotional Problems scale | varies | — |
| CDI 2:T (Teacher) | Functional Problems scale | varies | — |

Both yield Total Score and two scale scores; subscale scores are available only for the self-report form.

## 4. Converting Raw Scores to T-Scores

Raw scores are converted to **T-scores** (M = 50, SD = 10) using age- and gender-stratified normative tables provided in the CDI manual (Kovacs, 1992) or CDI 2 manual (Kovacs & MHS Staff, 2011).

**General interpretation of T-scores:**

| T-Score Range | Interpretation |
|---------------|---------------|
| < 55 | Average |
| 55–59 | Mildly elevated |
| 60–64 | Moderately elevated |
| 65–69 | Elevated |
| ≥ 70 | Very elevated (clinically significant) |

T‑score conversion is essential for comparing results across forms (self, parent, teacher) and across age/gender groups.

---

## 5. Cutoff Scores for Screening

The CDI manual (Kovacs, 1992) recommends:

| Population | Recommended Cutoff (raw total score) | Reference |
|------------|--------------------------------------|-----------|
| General / community screening | ≥ 19–20 | Kovacs, 1992; Bang et al., 2015 |
| Clinic-referred samples | ≥ 13 | Kovacs, 1992 |
| Severity (community) — Mild | ≥ 15 | Bang et al., 2015 |
| Severity (community) — Moderate | ≥ 20 | Bang et al., 2015 |
| Severity (community) — Severe | ≥ 25 | Bang et al., 2015 |

At the general-population cutoff of 20, the CDI shows sensitivity of approximately 0.83 and specificity of approximately 0.89 (Bang et al., 2015). For the CDI 2 short form (CDI 2:SR[S]), a cutoff of ≥ 10 provides comparable sensitivity (0.82) and specificity (0.93) (Kim et al., 2022).

**Caution:** Cutoff scores vary across cultures. Clinicians should consult locally validated norms when available. A positive screen must be followed by a structured clinical interview (e.g., K-SADS) to confirm diagnosis (Fristad et al., 1997; Bang et al., 2015).

---

## 6. Multi-Rater Interpretation

The CDI 2 is designed for multi-rater assessment. Discrepancies between self-report, parent, and teacher forms are common and should be interpreted clinically rather than as measurement error. The CDI 2 manual provides guidelines for comparing T-scores across raters using discrepancy tables.

---

## 7. Handling Missing Items

Per the CDI 2 manual:
- If **fewer than 20%** of items are missing on a scale/subscale, prorate the score (mean of completed items × number of items in scale).
- If **20% or more** items are missing, the scale/subscale score should not be computed.
- For the **Total Score**, this typically means up to 5 missing items can be prorated on the full-length form.
